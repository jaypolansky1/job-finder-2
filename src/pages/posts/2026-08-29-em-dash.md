This post demonstrates the em dash — the long dash used like this — and checks rendering in Astro.

Examples:
- Inline: here is an em dash — see it?
- Paired use: New sentence — another sentence.
- With text on both sides — and no spaces around the dash (common style).

If you see long dashes above (—) then em dashes are rendering correctly.
